Kanga Cookie and Tracking Technologies Policy

Effective date: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}
Last updated: {{EFFECTIVE_DATE}}

The Kanga app (the "App") does not use web browser cookies. It uses similar technologies: identifiers created by software development kits (SDKs), the Android Advertising ID, and the App's own local storage. This policy explains what they are, why we use them and how you can control them. It forms part of our Privacy Policy.

1. Definitions

  • Tracking technologies: any technology that stores information on your device or reads information already stored there, including cookies, SDK identifiers and advertising IDs.
  • First-party: stored by us inside the App.
  • Third-party: stored or read by another company's SDK, such as Google.
  • Strictly necessary: needed to provide a feature you asked for. These do not require consent under the EU ePrivacy Directive (Art. 5(3)) and similar laws.

3. Consent

  • EEA, UK and Switzerland: on first launch, the App shows Google's consent form (IAB Transparency & Consent Framework). Technologies marked "Yes" above are not used until you consent. You can use every feature of the App even if you refuse.
  • US states with privacy laws: you can opt out of the "sale" or "sharing" of personal data for targeted advertising (see Privacy Policy Section 12.2).
  • Republic of Korea: personalized ads are used only if you tick the optional consent on the first-launch screen (unticked by default). Otherwise the App requests only non-personalized ads. You can withdraw at any time in Settings > Personalized ads.
  • Other regions: you can refuse personalized ads at any time using the methods below.

4. How to manage your choices

  1. In the App: go to Settings > Privacy choices to review, change or withdraw consent, or to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal data. This menu is shown in regions where it applies (such as the EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws). Users in Korea can turn personalized ads on or off in Settings > Personalized ads. Withdrawing is as easy as giving consent.
  2. Advertising ID: go to Android Settings > Google > Ads and tap Delete advertising ID (menu names vary by device and Android version). Apps will then no longer be able to use it for personalized ads.
  3. Clear App data: go to Android Settings > Apps > Kanga > Storage > Clear data, or uninstall the App. This removes first-party storage and SDK identifiers, and also deletes your in-App library and settings.
  4. Google account: manage ad personalization at My Ad Center.
  5. Industry opt-outs: Your Online Choices (EU) · AppChoices (US, DAA).

Refusing personalized ads does not reduce the number of ads; you will see ads that are not based on your interests.

5. Third-party policies

Third parties process data under their own policies: Google Privacy Policy · How Google uses data in ads · Firebase privacy.
