This Privacy Policy explains how {{OPERATOR_NAME}} ("we", "us") processes personal data when you use the Kanga mobile application for Android (the "App"). It applies worldwide. Section 12 adds the information that specific laws require for users in certain regions (EEA/UK/Switzerland, the United States, Brazil, the Republic of Korea and others).

For the purposes of the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR"), the Korean Personal Information Protection Act ("PIPA") and similar laws, we are the controller of the personal data described here.

1. Summary

  • No account, no login. We do not ask for your name, email address or phone number to use the App.
  • No own servers. We do not operate servers that store your data. Media you download, links you paste, your library and your Hidden Vault stay on your device and are never sent to us.
  • Google services. The App uses Google AdMob (ads), Google Firebase (analytics, crash reporting, notifications, remote configuration) and Google Play Billing (payments). These services process device identifiers and usage data as described below.
  • Your choice. Where the law requires consent (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland), we ask for it before using analytics or advertising identifiers. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Privacy choices in the App (shown in regions where it applies, such as the EEA, UK, Switzerland and US states with privacy laws).
  • Personalized ads in Korea only with your consent. For users in the Republic of Korea, the App shows personalized ads only if you give separate, optional consent on the first-launch screen; otherwise it requests only non-personalized ads. You can change this anytime in Settings > Personalized ads.
  • We do not sell your personal data for money. Some US state laws treat personalized advertising as "selling" or "sharing"; see Section 12.2 for how to opt out.

We do not send links you paste, file names, search terms or the content you download to Firebase Analytics or any other analytics service.

Data processed only on your device

  • Country check. The App reads the country of your SIM card and mobile network and your device's time zone on your device only to decide whether to show the optional personalized-ads consent and whether to request non-personalized ads (see Section 12.4). This information is not transmitted by us.
  • Clipboard contents. The App reads the clipboard on your device only to detect a link you copied. Clipboard contents are not transmitted. The last link the App processed is kept in the App's local storage so the same link is not analyzed twice.
  • Your downloaded media, links, library and vault. These are stored only on your device.

Data we do NOT collect

  • Payment card details. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play. We never receive your card number or billing address.
  • Biometric data. App Lock and the Hidden Vault use Android's built-in device authentication (fingerprint, face or device PIN/pattern). The App only receives a "success" or "failure" result. Your biometric data never leaves your device's secure hardware.
  • Precise location, contacts, photos you did not save through the App, microphone or camera data.

Connections to third-party platforms

  • Downloading. When you download, the App connects directly from your device to the public page of the relevant platform (for example TikTok or Instagram). No intermediate server of ours is involved. That platform may process your IP address and other data under its own privacy policy; we do not receive this data.
  • Download engine updates. The App periodically checks GitHub (github.com, operated by GitHub, Inc.) for updates to its open-source download engine (yt-dlp) and downloads them directly. GitHub receives your IP address and basic request data under its own privacy statement. No personal data is sent by us.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have some or all of these rights:

How to exercise your rights

  • Instantly in the App or device: change your choices in Settings > Privacy choices (where shown) or Settings > Personalized ads (Korea); reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android settings; clear the App's data or uninstall it.
  • By email: write to {{CONTACT_EMAIL}}. We respond free of charge within 10 days (Republic of Korea), 1 month (EEA/UK, extendable by 2 months for complex requests with notice), 45 days (US states, extendable once by 45 days), 15 days (Brazil), or the period your local law requires.

Identifying you

Because the App has no accounts, we cannot find your data by name or email. Please include the Support ID shown at the bottom of the App's Settings screen (long-press to copy) and/or your Google Play order ID (starts with "GPA.") so we can locate the data linked to your installation. The Support ID is shown only while analytics is enabled; if it is not shown, no analytics data is linked to your installation. If we cannot identify you, we will tell you (GDPR Art. 11).

You may use an authorized agent; we may ask for proof of authorization. Legal guardians may exercise rights on behalf of children. We will not discriminate against you for exercising your rights. Purchase records that we must keep by law cannot be deleted until the retention period ends; we will tell you if this applies.
