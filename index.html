Keeply

A simple Android app for saving and organizing the videos and photos you are allowed to keep.

  • Save from a linkPaste or share a link and keep the media in your own library.
  • Stays on your deviceYour library is stored only on your phone. No account needed.
  • Keeply VIPNo ads, app lock, a hidden vault and automatic gallery saving.

Keeply is an independent app and is not affiliated with any social media platform.
Please save only content you created or have permission to save.
